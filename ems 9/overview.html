01 The loop

Four stages, one continuous cycle

Every feature in VoltGrid serves one of these four moves. Read consumption, price it, collect it, and resolve whatever goes wrong along the way.

01🔌

Read the meter

Metered units are entered against a customer's connection and tariff mode.

02🧾

Generate the bill

The engine applies live slab rates and a service charge, itemised in real time.

03💳

Take the payment

The customer pays by card or UPI; the bill flips to Paid and a receipt is stored.

04📮

Close the complaint

Issues are logged, routed to staff, and tracked to resolution with a full timeline.

Access model

Two accounts, three staff positions

There is no separate admin login. Administrative power lives inside the employee role and is decided entirely by a person's position. Positions are tiered, and the navigation, page guards and buttons all read from the tier — so each person sees only what their position allows.

TIER 3

Grid Manager

Field Operations
  • Sets tariffs & policies
  • Generates bills, manages customers
  • Manages staff & the complaint board
  • Routes complaints to anyone below
TIER 2

Supervisor

Billing Support
  • Runs billing & customer accounts
  • Works the full complaint board
  • Routes complaints to field agents
  • No policy or staff management
TIER 1

Field Agent

Line Maintenance
  • Works only their assigned complaints
  • Looks up customer accounts in the field
  • Cannot route complaints onward

02 Complaint lifecycle

From logged to resolved, every step recorded

Each complaint carries an append-only update log, so the customer and staff see the same timeline. Status moves in one direction along these stages.

OPEN

Registered

Customer submits an issue — an entry is written to the update log.

ASSIGNED

Routed to staff

A manager or supervisor hands it to a lower-tier employee.

IN PROGRESS

Being worked

The assigned agent updates status and adds field notes.

RESOLVED

Closed out

Marked resolved with a note; the full timeline stays visible to the customer.

Under the hood

How it's built

Deliberately simple: every page is an HTML file with its own small script, and they all share one data module. No build step, no dependencies.

js/data.js

The single source of truth

One EMS object holds all customers, staff, bills, complaints and policies — plus every function that reads or changes them. State persists in localStorage.

js/chrome.js

Position-driven furniture

Builds the staff top-bar from the signed-in employee's position, so each person only sees links they're allowed — and adds the shared footer to every page.

requireRole / requirePerm

Page guards

Each screen checks who's logged in and whether they hold the needed capability, bouncing a field agent away from a manager-only page.

css/ · vanilla JS

No framework

style.css, components.css and landing.css carry the theme; forms use a tiny validation.js helper. That's the whole stack.
