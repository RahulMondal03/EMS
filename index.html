Frequently asked questions

How do I register for an account?

Click "Get Started" and fill in your personal details along with your meter number. Once verified, you'll receive login credentials to access the customer portal.

What payment methods are supported?

We support credit cards, debit cards, and net banking. All transactions are processed through a secure payment gateway with instant confirmation.

How do I track my complaint status?

After registering a complaint, you'll receive a tracking ID. Use it in the "Complaint Status" section of your dashboard to see real-time updates from the assigned technician.

Can I view my past bills and payment history?

Yes. The customer portal maintains a complete history of all your bills, payments, and receipts. You can download them as PDF at any time.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. VoltGrid uses role-based access control, encrypted data transmission, and secure authentication to ensure your information is protected at all times.
